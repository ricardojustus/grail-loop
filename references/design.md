# Domain pack — Design (posters, decks, icons, identities, static pieces)

Design is the purest ceiling domain: thin Contract, almost everything lives in the
blind A/B. It is also the fastest loop — cycles are cheap, so run more of
them with tighter critique.

## Contract patterns (floor)

- Deliverable renders at final size/resolution; correct color space; correct
  format(s) for the destination (print bleed, @2x/@3x exports, .icns sizes).
- Legibility floors: minimum type size at viewing distance, WCAG contrast for
  anything functional, small-size legibility test for icons (render at 16px
  and 32px, verify recognizable).
- All required variants exist (dark/light, sizes, aspect ratios) — enumerate
  and check.
- Text content proofed: no lorem ipsum remnants, no typos (script-check
  against the copy source).
- Original work: no reproduction of existing artworks or copyrighted
  characters; refs are for comparison only.

## Critic roster (ceiling)

- **Visual critic** — champion–challenger comparison + master A/B. Suggested dimensions:
  composition/eye-path, hierarchy, palette discipline, typographic craft
  (spacing, pairing, rag), concept clarity (does the idea read in 2
  seconds?), craft finish (edges, alignment, optical corrections).
- **Squint critic** — evaluates at thumbnail size and at a blurred render:
  does the hierarchy survive? Is there one dominant read? (Machine-assisted:
  generate the downscaled/blurred variants for the critic.)
- **Brief critic** (the systems critic) — cold-reads the original brief, then
  the piece: does the piece answer the brief, or is it beautiful and off-
  target? Fails rounds that drift from intent regardless of craft.

## Bar library (unreachable masters by dimension)

- Poster composition: the acknowledged masters of the genre being attempted
  (Saul Bass lineage for reductive concept posters; Swiss/International style
  for typographic systems; contemporary A24-tier key art for cinematic).
- Editorial/typographic craft: canonical magazine and book design.
- Icon craft: Apple's own current-language app icons; the acknowledged best
  of the platform's ecosystem.
- Deck/keynote craft: Apple keynote slides; top-tier agency decks.
- Cross-media: film frames for mood/palette bars; master paintings for light
  and composition — always as comparison targets, never as material to copy.

Directions (reachable — never in the bar set): a specific existing style the
piece should speak ("in the vein of mid-century travel posters") — vocabulary
for the builders, not a target for the critics.

## Notes

- Cycles are cheap here, but the plateau standard does NOT tighten — the
  minimum (4 cycles) and window (3) stay. What changes is what cheapness
  buys: more cycles in the same sitting, and fan-out breadth — generate
  2–3 divergent candidates per cycle and let the champion–challenger
  comparison pick before refining.
- Blind comparisons between the run's own divergent candidates are legal and
  cheap — an internal tournament feeding the champion ladder.
